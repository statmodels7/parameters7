# Log-Determinant of an Autoregressive Parameter

Closed form from the innovation variances: \\p\log\gamma_0 + \sum_k
(p-k)\log(1 - r_k^2)\\. No factorisation and no determinant is computed.

## Arguments

- s:

  An
  [`AutoregressiveParam`](https://statmodels7.github.io/parameters7/reference/AutoregressiveParam.md)
  object.

- eta:

  A numeric vector of free values.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A single number.
