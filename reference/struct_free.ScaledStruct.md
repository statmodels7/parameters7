# Free Vector of a Scaled Structure

The scale read off the ratio to the fixed matrix, after checking that
the matrix really is a multiple of it. The ratio is taken at the entry
of \\P\\ of largest magnitude, which is where it is best determined.

## Arguments

- s:

  A
  [`ScaledStruct`](https://statmodels7.github.io/covstructs7/reference/ScaledStruct.md)
  object.

- m:

  A symmetric matrix.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A named numeric vector of free values.
