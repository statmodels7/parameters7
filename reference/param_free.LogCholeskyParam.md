# Free Vector of a Log-Cholesky Parameter

The Cholesky factor of the matrix, with its diagonal logged. Exact, and
the inverse of
[`param_value`](https://statmodels7.github.io/parameters7/reference/param_value.md)
because the factor with a positive diagonal is unique.

## Arguments

- s:

  A
  [`LogCholeskyParam`](https://statmodels7.github.io/parameters7/reference/LogCholeskyParam.md)
  object.

- m:

  A symmetric positive definite matrix.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A named numeric vector of free values.
