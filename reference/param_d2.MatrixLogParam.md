# Second Derivatives of a Matrix Logarithm Parameter

The second Frechet derivative: chains of two rotated directions against
three-point divided differences, summed over the two orderings.

## Arguments

- s:

  A
  [`MatrixLogParam`](https://statmodels7.github.io/parameters7/reference/MatrixLogParam.md)
  object.

- eta:

  A numeric vector of free values.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A named list of symmetric matrices.
