# Log-Determinant of a Sum of Fixed Matrices

The log-determinant of the assembled matrix, or its log
pseudo-determinant over the non-zero eigenvalues when the family is rank
deficient.

## Arguments

- s:

  A
  [`SumStructParam`](https://statmodels7.github.io/parameters7/reference/SumStructParam.md)
  object.

- eta:

  A numeric vector of length `s@n_free`.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A single number.
