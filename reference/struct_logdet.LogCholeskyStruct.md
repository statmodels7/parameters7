# Log-Determinant of a Log-Cholesky Structure

Closed form and linear in the free vector: \\\log\|M\| = 2 \sum_i \log
L\_{ii}\\, which is twice the sum of the free values on the diagonal. No
factorisation and no determinant is computed.

## Arguments

- s:

  A
  [`LogCholeskyStruct`](https://statmodels7.github.io/covstructs7/reference/LogCholeskyStruct.md)
  object.

- eta:

  A numeric vector of free values.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A single number.
