# Log-Determinant of a Matrix Logarithm Parameter

Closed form and linear: \\\log\|M\| = \mathrm{tr}(S)\\, the sum of the
diagonal free values.

## Arguments

- s:

  A
  [`MatrixLogParam`](https://statmodels7.github.io/parameters7/reference/MatrixLogParam.md)
  object.

- eta:

  A numeric vector of free values.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A single number.
