# Second Derivatives of a Simplex Parameter

Closed form, one further product rule.

## Arguments

- s:

  A
  [`SimplexParam`](https://statmodels7.github.io/parameters7/reference/SimplexParam.md)
  object.

- eta:

  A numeric vector of free values.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A named list of vectors of length \\K\\.
