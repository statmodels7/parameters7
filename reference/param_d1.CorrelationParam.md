# First Derivatives of a Correlation Parameter

Closed form by the Leibniz rule on \\R = LL^\top\\, the factor's
derivatives coming from the trigonometric tables of the angles.

## Arguments

- s:

  A
  [`CorrelationParam`](https://statmodels7.github.io/parameters7/reference/CorrelationParam.md)
  object.

- eta:

  A numeric vector of free values.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A named list of symmetric matrices with a zero diagonal, the diagonal of
the value being constant.
