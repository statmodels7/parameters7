# Derivatives of a Compound Symmetry Parameter

Closed form at every order: the value is the scale times a pattern
linear in the correlation, so a component with \\a\\ scale indices and
\\b\\ correlation indices is one derivative of each.

## Arguments

- s:

  A
  [`CompoundSymmetryParam`](https://statmodels7.github.io/parameters7/reference/CompoundSymmetryParam.md)
  object.

- eta:

  A numeric vector of two free values.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A named list of symmetric matrices.
