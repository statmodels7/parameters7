# First Derivatives of a Matrix Logarithm Parameter

The Frechet derivative of the exponential by Daleckii-Krein: the rotated
direction weighted entrywise by first divided differences of \\e^x\\ at
the eigenvalues.

## Arguments

- s:

  A
  [`MatrixLogParam`](https://statmodels7.github.io/parameters7/reference/MatrixLogParam.md)
  object.

- eta:

  A numeric vector of free values.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A named list of symmetric matrices.
