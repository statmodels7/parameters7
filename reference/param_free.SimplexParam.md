# Free Vector of a Simplex Parameter

The additive log-ratio, exact: \\\eta_a = \log(\pi_a/\pi_K)\\. Rejected
outside the open simplex or when the vector does not sum to one.

## Arguments

- s:

  A
  [`SimplexParam`](https://statmodels7.github.io/parameters7/reference/SimplexParam.md)
  object.

- m:

  A probability vector of length \\K\\.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A named numeric vector of free values.
