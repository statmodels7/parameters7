# Log-Determinant of a Scaled Structure

Closed form: \\r \log h(\eta) + \log\|P\|\_+\\, with \\r\\ the rank and
the second term a constant of the structure. This is the log-determinant
when the family is of full rank and the log pseudo-determinant
otherwise, in one expression.

## Arguments

- s:

  A
  [`ScaledStruct`](https://statmodels7.github.io/covstructs7/reference/ScaledStruct.md)
  object.

- eta:

  A numeric vector of free values.

- ...:

  Unused.

## Value

A single number.
